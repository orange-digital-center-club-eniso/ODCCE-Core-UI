WHO WE ARE

Empowering Tomorrow's Engineers Today

Founded in 2017 at the National Engineering School of Sousse (ENISo), Orange Digital Center Club ENISo (ODCCE) is a student-led innovation hub operating under the support of Orange Digital Center Tunisia. The club serves as a hands-on sandbox bridging academic theory with industry demands through specialized divisions, expert mentorship, and technical workshops.

Our Mission

Preparing members for industry standards through advanced technical workshops, soft skills training, certified programs, intensive bootcamps, and expert-led projects.

Our Vision

Fostering a thriving ecosystem where technical mastery meets essential interpersonal skills, transforming students into well-rounded tech leaders.

Core Values

Driven by hands-on engineering, continuous teamwork, public speaking confidence, high technical standards, and impactful community growth.
